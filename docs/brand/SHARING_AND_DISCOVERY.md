# Sugo AI sharing and discovery

Last reviewed: August 14, 2026

This guide records the canonical domain, search metadata, social-card system,
and external setup needed to keep Sugo AI consistent when links are shared or
discovered.

## Canonical domain

The canonical website is `https://sugoai.com` without `www`.

Every public page declares an apex-domain canonical URL and the sitemap contains
only apex-domain URLs. `next.config.ts` also includes a permanent `www` redirect,
but it can run only after `www.sugoai.com` points to the same deployment.

## Public discovery pages

| Path | Search title | Social title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | AI Product Development for Traditional Businesses \| Sugo AI | Modern product development for companies that aren’t software companies. |
| `/how-we-work` | Product Development From Discovery Through Production \| Sugo AI | We run like a software company. That’s where we come from. |
| `/about` | Product Studio for Traditional Businesses \| Sugo AI | A product studio for traditional businesses building serious software. |
| `/contact` | Start a Conversation \| Sugo AI | If the work matters, the conversation is worth having. |

The client-specific `/tmc` and exploratory `/tmc-example` routes are explicitly
marked `noindex`. The writing routes remain `noindex` while their content is
gated.

## Social cards

Each public page has a deterministic 1200 × 630 Open Graph image generated by
Next.js. The cards use:

- Red Hat Display for the primary statement;
- JetBrains Mono for labels and service metadata;
- the canonical transparent tomato mark;
- the Viola Light palette; and
- page-specific brand language rather than search-oriented titles.

The shared renderer lives in `src/lib/og-image.tsx`. Route-specific image entry
points live beside the relevant pages as `opengraph-image.tsx`.

## Search endpoints

- `/robots.txt` allows public crawling and points to the sitemap.
- `/sitemap.xml` lists the four active public discovery pages.
- `/manifest.webmanifest` describes browser colors and application icons.
- The homepage publishes Organization and WebSite structured data.

## Icon set

- `/favicon.ico` supports legacy browser tabs.
- `/favicon-48.png` supplies a search-compatible square favicon.
- `/icon-192.png` and `/icon-512.png` support manifests and larger surfaces.
- `/apple-icon.png` supports Apple touch surfaces.

## External launch checklist

1. Point `www.sugoai.com` to the current application and verify that every path
   permanently redirects to the equivalent apex-domain path.
2. Add and verify the apex-domain property in Google Search Console.
3. Submit `https://sugoai.com/sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console and Bing
   Webmaster Tools.
4. Request indexing for the homepage, How we work, and About pages.
5. Test shared URLs with LinkedIn Post Inspector and the major messaging apps
   used by the team.
6. Add verified LinkedIn profile and company-page URLs to the Organization
   structured data once those URLs are final.
7. Re-enable writing routes in the sitemap only after the placeholder article
   bodies are replaced and `WRITING_ENABLED` is turned on.
