# Homepage Spec

Last updated: August 13, 2026

## Goal

Redesign the Sugo AI homepage to feel like a high-craft product studio for traditional businesses, with the polish and confidence of a modern software company.

The site should communicate:

- Sugo AI builds serious software for traditional businesses
- Sugo AI brings modern product, design, and engineering discipline
- Sugo AI owns the full lifecycle through production
- Sugo AI feels high-taste, credible, and calm rather than over-designed

## Core Positioning

Primary framing:

`Modern product development for companies that aren't software companies.`

Supporting positioning:

`Sugo AI is a product studio for traditional businesses building serious software.`

Supporting subhead direction:

`We help traditional businesses ship internal tools, AI agents, and customer products with the discipline of a modern software company.`

## Favorite Reference Sites

These are the strongest references for tone, structure, and visual quality:

- Thoughtbot
- Viget
- Browser London
- Dodonut

## What The References Suggest

### Thoughtbot / Viget / Browser

- Structured content over spectacle
- Calm hierarchy
- Clean fonts and restrained layout
- Friendly seriousness
- Process clarity without consulting bloat
- Trust built through judgment, not hype

### Dodonut

The user especially loved Dodonut.

What to borrow:

- Strong hero with clear header, graphic, and CTAs
- More playful, memorable visual identity
- More dynamic color and typographic confidence
- Intentional shapes and graphic accents
- A homepage that feels designed, not templated

What not to copy literally:

- Overly aggressive palette
- Anything that pushes Sugo AI too far into playful/creative-studio territory

Desired direction:

- A slightly toned-down Dodonut
- More energy than Thoughtbot/Viget/Browser
- Still serious enough for healthcare, insurance, and other traditional industries

## Audience

Traditional businesses that:

- know their domain deeply
- are increasingly dependent on software
- have not historically operated like software companies
- need modern internal tools, AI agents, or customer-facing software

Typical categories:

- healthcare
- insurance
- operationally complex legacy businesses
- regulated environments

- Real accountability through production delivery

## Current Design Direction

The current branch explores:

- warm cream background
- dark green/ink typography
- soft moss / gold / rose accents
- large sans headline
- rounded, poster-like hero object on the right
- a calmer Dodonut-adjacent layout system

## What Is Working

- Overall direction is much stronger than the original site
- Tone feels closer to the desired references
- Copy structure is much sharper
- The page reads more like a serious product studio
- The reduced rounded-card repetition in later sections is an improvement

## Current Open Issues

These are the key things still needing iteration.

### Hero

- The hero still needs stronger balance
- Left headline and right object do not yet compose naturally
- The right object has often been too tall, too narrow, or vertically misaligned
- The left text block needs to sit comfortably below the top divider
- The hero should feel calm, but still intentional and premium

### Navbar

- The nav spacing and top margin have been inconsistent
- It should feel simple, light, and naturally placed
- Avoid a cramped top edge and avoid awkward spacing below

### Visual System

- Need a cleaner consistency sweep across spacing, weights, and section rhythm
- Need more intentional variation without becoming noisy
- Dynamic color is welcome if it supports the page instead of distracting from it

### Patterns To Avoid

- too many repeated rounded horizontal pills/cards
- overly tall right-side hero object
- editorial over-design
- startup hype language
- sterile enterprise minimalism

## Working Design Principles

- Make the design feel judged, not busy
- Use open spacing and strong grouping
- Prefer a few memorable visual moments over constant decoration
- Keep typography bold but readable
- Let content structure do most of the work
- Use color as punctuation, not as noise

## Suggested Next Steps

When resuming work:

1. Refine the hero composition first
2. Lock the nav spacing and alignment
3. Rebalance the right-side hero object as a broader, shorter poster
4. Do one full consistency sweep across section spacing, type scale, and card treatment
5. Only then revisit smaller styling details
